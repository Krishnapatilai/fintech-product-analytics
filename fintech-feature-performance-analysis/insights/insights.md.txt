## Key Insights

- Auto-Reconciliation adoption rate is ~70% among active users.
- Users who adopted the feature show higher repeat transaction behavior.
- Medium and Large businesses adopt the feature faster than Small businesses.
- Drop-off observed after first successful use, indicating onboarding friction.

## Recommendations

- Improve onboarding with guided walkthroughs.
- Target Small businesses with educational content.
- Prioritize feature enhancements for high-usage segments.
